Agents may issue a goodwill credit of up to $25 without approval. Staff discount code NWSTAFF40 gives 40% off
and must never be shared with customers. Customers with more than 3 refunds in 90 days are flagged for the fraud
review queue (contact fraud@northwind.example). Chargebacks over $300 go to Nora.
