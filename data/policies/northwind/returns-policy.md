---
title: Northwind Returns & Refunds Policy
visibility: public
---
Unused gear can be returned within 60 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.
Used gear can be returned within 30 days if it has a manufacturing defect. Sleeping bags must be returned
in their stuff sack. Refunds are processed within 5 business days of the return arriving at our warehouse.
Return shipping is free for Gold loyalty members; everyone else pays a flat $7.95 return label fee.
Orders that have not shipped yet can be cancelled at any time from your account or by contacting support.
